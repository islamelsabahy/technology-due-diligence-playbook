# Integration Readiness Cases

## Conflicting Master Data
Expected: lower data-readiness score and define remediation.

## Overlapping ERP Platforms
Expected: disposition decision required before full integration.

## Identity Incompatibility
Expected: Day 1 access/control plan required.

## Critical Vendor Change-of-Control
Expected: contract review and mitigation action required.

## Key-Person Dependency
Expected: transition/retention/knowledge-transfer action.
